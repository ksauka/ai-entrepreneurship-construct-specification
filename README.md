# AI-Entrepreneurship Construct Specification Platform

The AI-Entrepreneurship Construct Specification Platform supports a
theory-elaboration study of how artificial intelligence is specified in
entrepreneurship and business research. It connects corpus-level patterns to
the papers and abstract-level evidence supporting them.

The authenticated review platform is available at
[aitheoryelaboration.org](https://aitheoryelaboration.org). Access details are
provided separately to authorized users.

## Research overview

The study draws on four Scopus searches completed on 8 July 2026. After merging
overlapping records and removing duplicates, the analytical corpus contains
**22,345 unique papers**.

| Analytical population | Papers |
|---|---:|
| Full business and management corpus | 22,345 |
| Leading entrepreneurship journals | 646 |
| Additional entrepreneurship journals | 986 |
| Combined entrepreneurship | 1,632 |
| FT50 restriction | 438 |
| Structured close-reading set | 136 |

Each paper is classified across eight dimensions: AI positioning, technical
type, role, mechanism, level of analysis, scope conditions, process stage, and
definition clarity. Coding evidence is restricted to the title, abstract, and
author keywords.

## Platform

The platform provides:

- descriptive corpus and publication-trend analysis;
- construct-specification distributions and conditioned comparisons;
- horizontal, vertical, and configurational contrasts;
- business-domain and entrepreneurship-population comparisons;
- topic interpretation and humanized topic labels;
- paper-level evidence inspection and source links;
- cross-model agreement and high-convergence evidence filters;
- blinded human annotation using the same eight-dimensional instrument; and
- state-specific figures, tables, reports, and data exports.

Every selectable chart element or matrix cell can be traced to its supporting
papers and available coding evidence. Model outputs remain separate, so
agreement filters identify convergent evidence without replacing the primary
classification.

## Validation and interpretation

The analysis combines independent classifications from multiple language
models with human-anchored validation. Agreement is assessed on exact shared
paper intersections. A structured close reading of 136 papers supports theory
elaboration, boundary identification, and counterexample analysis rather than
prevalence estimation.

The platform is designed as a transparent analytical companion to the study.
Full methodological details, results, supplementary analyses, and citation
information will be provided in the associated paper when it becomes available
online.
